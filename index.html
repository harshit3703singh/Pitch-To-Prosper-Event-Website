About the Event

Organized by the ABES Institute of Technology (ABESIT), Ghaziabad.

Our core objective is to accelerate promising ideas from conceptualization toward validation, formal incubation, seed investment, strategic industry collaboration, and sustainable scaling.

Selected startups secure direct opportunities to present before elite investors, display functional prototypes, receive constructive mentorship, explore funding, and expand professional networks.

Why Participate?

"A visionary idea requires more than technical innovation—it demands a robust ecosystem to scale."

We establish a bridge between creators and key stakeholders who drive market entry and commercial expansion.

Participants receive unparalleled exposure through live investor sessions, dedicated exhibition spaces, technical panel reviews, and direct networking opportunities.

Who Can Apply?

Inviting innovators, student disruptors, and founders across all developmental phases.

✔ Early-stage Startups looking for Seed or Pre-Seed capital funding
✔ Growth-stage Enterprises seeking Series A/B expansion funding
✔ Student Entrepreneurs possessing validated working prototypes
✔ Researchers and Academicians aiming to commercialize proprietary IP
Eligible Sectors: Open across all domains (AgriTech, FinTech, EdTech, HealthTech, AI/ML, Clean Energy, etc.)
Team Size Requirement: Each participating team must consist of 4 to 6 members.
Registration Fee: A nominal registration fee of ₹500 per team is applicable.

Pitch Deck Guidelines

Selected candidates receive 5 to 7 minutes for presentation followed by a 3 to 5 minute interactive Q&A session with the investor jury.

Standard Structure: Problem Statement → Solution → Core Technology → Target Market Size → Revenue Model → Competitive Advantage → Current Traction → Go-to-Market Strategy → Management Team → Funding Ask & Use of Funds

About ABES Institute of Technology

ABES Institute of Technology (ABESIT), Ghaziabad, is an premier technical institution established in 2007 under the Ved Foundation Trust. Committed to academic excellence and technical rigor, the institute is approved by AICTE, affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, and holds NBA accreditation for its primary engineering programs.

Situated on a modern 17-acre campus near Crossing Republik on NH-24, Ghaziabad, ABESIT offers high-grade professional education across B.Tech, BBA, BCA, and MCA disciplines, featuring specialized streams in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication, and Mechanical Engineering.

With an exceptional placement footprint featuring tier-1 multinational partners such as TCS, Amazon, Wipro, Infosys, and IBM, ABESIT also houses a dedicated NVIDIA Centre of Excellence focused on high-performance Artificial Intelligence education and research.
